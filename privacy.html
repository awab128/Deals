Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Visitors of the website

We do not ask for your name, email or any account. The site uses GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly visit counter without cookies, to count page views. Clicking a store link takes you to that store, whose own privacy policy applies.

TikTok app ("deals")

The app is used only by the site owner to publish videos to the owner's own TikTok account. When the owner connects TikTok, TikTok gives the app an access token and basic profile information (user name, display name). These are stored only on the owner's computer, used only to upload the owner's videos, and never shared or sold. No data of other TikTok users is collected. The connection can be removed at any time in TikTok's settings (Security > Manage app permissions).
